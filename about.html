Our Story

Mount Isa's leading detailing studio

TB Detailing is run by Tyler Briggs from a purpose-built studio at 42 Barkly Highway, Miles End. What started as an obsession with the perfect finish is now the North-West's go-to for ceramic coating, paint protection and detailing.

Out here, vehicles work harder than anywhere in the country — red dust, 40-degree summers and thousands of highway kilometres. That's why we back every finish with certified products: System X ceramic coatings and Bushwrapz paint protection film, applied by a certified installer.

Cars, 4WDs, caravans, boats, trucks, planes, helicopters and mining equipment — if it has a finish worth protecting, it's welcome in the studio.

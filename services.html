Our Services

Superior protection. Unbeatable shine.

Every job is quoted individually — call or text 0420 605 919 and Tyler will size it up. All work by appointment, Mon–Fri 8am–4pm.

01

Ceramic Coating — System X Certified

Professional-grade System X ceramic protection bonds to your paint for years of hydrophobic, UV-resistant, chemical-resistant shine. The North-West sun is merciless — this is the armour.

  • Certified System X installer
  • Multi-year protection packages
  • Deep gloss that makes colour pop

02

Paint Protection Film — Bushwrapz

Self-healing film that takes the hits so your paint doesn't. Built for outback conditions — stone chips, scrub scratches and gravel rash stop here.

  • Bushwrapz certified installer
  • Bonnets, guards, bull bar surrounds or full fronts
  • Ideal for 4WDs and touring rigs

03

Window Tinting

Premium window film that knocks the heat and glare out of Mount Isa summers, protects your interior from UV fade and sharpens the look of any vehicle.

  • Heat, glare and 99% UV rejection
  • Cooler cabins, protected interiors
  • Cars, utes, trucks and machinery cabs

04

Paint Correction & Full Detailing

Swirl marks, scratches and faded paint machine-polished back to better-than-showroom, plus deep-cleaned interiors using premium, non-abrasive products.

  • Single and multi-stage paint correction
  • Full interior and exterior packages
  • Engine bays, wheels and trim restoration

05

Caravans & Boats

Big rigs deserve the same finish. Caravan wash, decontamination and protection packages, plus marine detailing that handles everything from tinnies to wakeboats.

  • Off-road caravan protection (ask about ceramic)
  • Hull, gelcoat and topside detailing
  • Pre-sale tidy-ups that add real value

06

Trucks, Aircraft & Mining Equipment

Mount Isa runs on heavy gear — and TB Detailing keeps it presentable. Truck and fleet washing, plane and helicopter detailing, and mining equipment cleaned to inspection standard.

  • Fleet and one-off truck detailing
  • Fixed-wing and rotary aircraft
  • Mining equipment and machinery
